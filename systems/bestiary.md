# Bestiary

31 NPC entries across 6 categories with kill milestones and discovery rewards.

**Access:** `/eg journal` (Bestiary tab) — Permission: `endgameqol.journal` (default: allow).

**v5.0.0 — Native Journal Page.** Bounty Board, Bestiary, and Achievements share a single native `.ui` page with 3 tabs. The Bestiary tab shows paginated mob cards, kill milestones, and discovery badges.

**Endgame Memories** — separate from the Bestiary, the in-game **Bench → Memories** tab includes a custom `Endgame` category with 19 of these NPCs exposed as unlockable Memory entries. Same creatures, different progression track.
