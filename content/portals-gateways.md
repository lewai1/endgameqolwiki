# Portals & Gateways

One block. Three keys. Three themed dungeons.

## Endgame Gateway

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lewai1/endgameqolwiki/main/images/icons/Endgame_Gateway.png" width="72" style="float:right;margin:0 0 8px 14px;">

The **Endgame Gateway** is a craftable portal block. Right-click it with an Endgame dungeon key to open the matching dungeon.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Rarity | Epic |
| Craft bench | Endgame Bench — Tier 1 |
| Recipe | 30 Adamantite Bar + 10 Cobalt Bar + 30 Fire Essence |
| Accepts | Void Realm Key · Frozen Dungeon Key · Swamp Dungeon Key |

Old **Shard of the Void** items are automatically renamed to **Void Realm Key** on connect — no player action required.

## How it works

1. **Craft & place** — craft the Endgame Gateway at the Endgame Bench and place it.
2. **Right-click with a key** — the block takes on the theme of your key (cyan, green, or purple) and glows.
3. **Walk in** — step into the portal to enter the dungeon.
4. **Return** — a <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lewai1/endgameqolwiki/main/images/icons/Portal_Return.png" width="28" style="vertical-align:middle;margin-right:4px;"> return portal inside the dungeon brings you back to the exact gateway you came from.
5. **Reusable** — when the last player leaves, the gateway goes back to its neutral state. Use any key again, as many times as you want.

A vanilla <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lewai1/endgameqolwiki/main/images/icons/Portal_Device.png" width="28" style="vertical-align:middle;margin-right:4px;"> Portal Device will not accept Endgame keys — a chat message redirects you to the Endgame Gateway.

## Temporal Portals are separate

**Temporal Portals** are particle-only and spawn randomly — they do not use gateway blocks or keys. See [Temporal Portals](temporal-portals).
