# Frozen Dungeon

## Flocon currency

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lewai1/endgameqolwiki/main/images/icons/Endgame_Flocon_Currency.png" width="40" style="float:left;margin:0 12px 6px 0;"> Flocons are the exclusive currency of the Frozen Dungeon. They drop from every frost mob in the dungeon and can be spent at Korvyn. Clear rooms systematically before visiting the trader or heading to the boss arena.

## Boss — Dragon Frost

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lewai1/endgameqolwiki/main/images/mobs/Dragon_Frost.png" width="120" style="float:right;margin:0 0 10px 16px;"> 3-phase fly/walk boss with **alternating immunity**. Detection range 25 blocks. Drops **Dragon Heart** + **Mithril Bars** (4–5) + bonus materials.

| Phase | HP threshold | Behavior | Counter |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 — Sky Sentinel | > 70 % | Flying. Melee-immune. | Bows, staves, spears |
| P2 — Ground Fury | 70 – 40 % | Grounded. Projectile-immune. | Melee weapons |
| P3 — Hybrid Frenzy | < 40 % | Cycles fly/ground every 8–10 s, alternating immunity | Both |

Attacks include Frost Bolt, Icy Wind (radial gust), Swing/Bite/Ground Slam (Stagger on Ground Slam), and Frost Breath (cone). A one-time chat hint fires when you hit the dragon with an immune damage type.

## After the dungeon

1. **Collect Dragon Heart** — guaranteed drop from Dragon Frost. Required to upgrade the Endgame Bench.
2. **Spend Flocons** — trade with Korvyn for Mithril armor, the Frostbite Blade, or potions.
3. **Craft Mithril gear** — use Mithril Bars from Dragon Frost and dungeon chests at the Endgame Bench.
4. **Enter the Swamp Dungeon** — with the upgraded bench, craft the Hedera Key and advance to the [Swamp Dungeon](swamp-dungeon).

## Block protection

Building is restricted inside the dungeon instance. Players cannot break or place blocks. Toggle: `EnableDungeonBlockProtection`.
