## Boss — Hedera

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lewai1/endgameqolwiki/main/images/mobs/Hedera.png" width="120" style="float:right;margin:0 0 10px 16px;"> Phased poison boss with Root AOE, Ground Slam, and a Charge lunge in the final phase.

| Phase | HP threshold | Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 — Vine Growth | > 66 % | Base kit: Poison Strike, Root AOE, Scream |
| P2 — Toxic Bloom | 66 – 33 % | Adds Vine Grab, Ground Slam (7-block AOE + Stagger), and lingering poison clouds every 18–25 s |
| P3 — Corrupted Fury | < 33 % | Adds the Charge lunge |

<div style="clear:both;"></div>

**Guaranteed & notable drops:**

<p>
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lewai1/endgameqolwiki/main/images/icons/Hedera_Gem.png" width="34" title="Hedera Gem" style="vertical-align:middle;margin-right:8px;">
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lewai1/endgameqolwiki/main/images/icons/Ingredient_Forest_Essence.png" width="34" title="Forest Essence ×10–15" style="vertical-align:middle;margin-right:8px;">
</p>

**Drops:** Hedera Gem, Onyxium Bars (3–7), Forest Essence (10–15), Void Essence (10–20), Swamp Currency (8–15), Voidheart, Hedera Seed accessory (~15%).

Bring antidotes for the poison phases. Dodge Ground Slam telegraphs and avoid the lingering clouds. The root phase locks you in place — watch for the visual telegraph.

See [Bosses & Elites — Hedera](bosses-elites) for the full attack table.

## Chest loot tiers

| Tier | Location | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common | Early rooms near the entrance | Potions, arrows, torches, food |
| Rare | Mid-dungeon combat rooms | Swamp Ingots, Swamp Gems, Onyxium Ore |
| Epic | Late-dungeon locked rooms | Onyxium Bars, rare materials, enchanted gear |
| Hidden | Behind breakable walls and underwater passages | Unique loot, bonus Swamp Currency |

## Block protection

Building is restricted inside the dungeon instance, preventing sequence-breaking. Toggle: `EnableDungeonBlockProtection`.
