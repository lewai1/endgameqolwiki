## Highlights

### Combat

- **Frozen Dungeon** — Dragon Frost with 3 HP-based phases (fly / ground cycling); melee-immune in P1, projectile-immune in P2.
- **Swamp Dungeon** — Hedera with Vine Grab, Ground Slam AOE, charge lunge, and lingering poison clouds from Phase 2.
- **Void Realm** — new dimension with a floating island arena. Golem Void + the 3-wave Void Gauntlet, dedicated combat music.

### Temporal portals

Random particle portals spawn every ~1h30 at 80–300 blocks. Three challenge arenas:

| Instance | Difficulty | Elite | Combat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Canopy Shrine | Chill | None | Exploration only — three themed chests |
| Oakwood Refuge | Mid-tier | Oakwood Warlord (1 000 HP) | Melee — battleaxe + scepter shot + ground slam |
| Eldergrove Hollow | Harder | Eldergrove Despot (1 800 HP) | Caster — Ice / Fire / Lightning + shield block |

### Pet companions

4 boss pets (one per boss + Dragon Fire). Feed to advance through 6 tiers (D → SS). Mount at Tier C, Tier S element wards, Tier SS auras, plus a Ghost / Equip mode that activates the perk without spawning the pet.

## Material progression

| Tier | Source | Notable on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adamantite | Vanilla survival cap | Starter endgame gear |
| Mithril | Frozen Dungeon, Oakwood Warlord, Eldergrove Despot | Mana regen on armor |
| Onyxium | Swamp Dungeon, Golem Void | HP regen on armor |
| Prisma | Golem Void only | Unique signature attacks on every weapon |

Prisma is exclusive to the Void Realm. Eldergrove and Oakwood drop Onyxium, never Prisma.
